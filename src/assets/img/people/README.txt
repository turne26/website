Real photos for the "How it works" live section (video tiles, founder screen, "You" marker).

Drop in up to 8 square-ish photos named person-1.jpg ... person-8.jpg (.jpg, .webp or .png).
Then run: python3 src/build.py
Any number that's missing falls back to the illustrated face.

Size around 400x400 px, under 60 KB each (export as .webp or compressed .jpg).
Use only photos you have the right to use commercially (your team or friends with
consent, or free-licence stock such as Unsplash or Pexels). Keep the
"Illustrative demo" note on the page.
